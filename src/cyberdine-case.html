Define Phase

Root-cause gate before ideation.

Root causes

01Blind & dated menus

Sold-out items, no photos, breakfast at dinner, 50% of elders blocked

02Awkward service gap

Waving for waiters; captains lose track of calls

03Rigid legacy POS

Prefix-only search, Wi-Fi drops, UPI erased tips

04Owner blind spots

Reprints, costly buzzers, no live insight

Diner
Avanthika & guests

Karthikeyan & partners

need live menu and pricing control, all-table billing oversight and wireless KOTs
